# Two Sides of the Coin
*Framework for evaluating trade-offs in engineering decisions.*

---

## 1️⃣ Philosophy

Every decision has **two perspectives**:

1. **Immediate Impact** → Speed, convenience, short-term deliverables  
2. **Long-Term Flow** → Maintainability, observability, operational resilience  

I balance both sides to ensure decisions **support platform flow**, not just immediate outputs.

---

## 2️⃣ Implementation Flow

1. **Identify the Trade-Off** → What are the short-term vs long-term consequences?  
2. **Assess Risks & Benefits** → Use metrics, standards, and Red/Green logic  
3. **Decide with Flow in Mind** → Prioritize maintainability, consistency, and observability  
4. **Document** → Capture rationale for future reference  

---

## 3️⃣ Principle

I treat every engineering choice as **a coin with two sides**: optimizing for speed without compromising flow and long-term platform integrity.
